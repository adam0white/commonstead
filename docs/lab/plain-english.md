# Player text

Use full grammatical sentences for instructions, speech and descriptions. Use articles and verbs. Short control labels and counted resource labels may be phrases.

Say literally what happens. Use one name for each thing. Explain a new game term on first use. Introduce one new idea at a time. Use familiar words for a reader who learned English as a second language. Instructions should aim at grade 7; no reading grade is claimed without measurement. Dialogue may be richer but must remain grammatical.

Describe uncertainty clearly. Keep rules, difficulty, names and culture. Remove words the player does not need. Keep stored simulation strings intact when replay requires them; use display projections. Errors must explain the failed action and the available next step. Keep technical detail in logs and exact lab mode.
